What Are The Ingredients In Gut Save?

Gut Save is formulated with 9 named ingredients — including Digestive Enzyme Blend, Probiotic Strains, Prebiotic Fiber, Ginger Root Extract, Peppermint Leaf Extract — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

1

Digestive Enzyme Blend

Enzyme activity is the backbone of most natural digestive formulas, supporting the breakdown of fats, proteins and carbohydrates so food moves through the gut more comfortably instead of fermenting and producing gas.

2

Probiotic Strains

Live beneficial bacteria are commonly included in digestive formulas to help maintain a balanced gut environment, which is associated with less bloating, better regularity and more comfortable digestion after meals.

3

Prebiotic Fiber

Prebiotic fiber acts as fuel for the good bacteria in the digestive tract, helping them thrive so the gut environment stays supportive rather than sluggish.

4

Ginger Root Extract

Ginger has a long traditional history of calming an unsettled stomach and easing the feeling of fullness or nausea after eating, making it a natural fit for a bloating-focused formula.

5

Peppermint Leaf Extract

Peppermint is well known for relaxing the muscles of the digestive tract, which can help relieve trapped gas, cramping and that tight, uncomfortable feeling after a meal.

6

Psyllium Husk

A gentle soluble fiber that absorbs water and adds bulk to stool, psyllium is one of the most studied natural ingredients for supporting comfortable, regular bowel movements.

7

Aloe Vera Extract

Aloe vera is used in digestive blends for its soothing properties, helping to settle irritation in the gut and support a calmer feeling after eating.

8

Fennel Seed Extract

Fennel seed is a traditional carminative, meaning it is used to help expel trapped gas and reduce the puffiness and distension that comes with bloating.

9

Licorice Root Extract

Licorice root has traditionally been used to soothe the stomach lining and support overall digestive comfort, particularly for people who feel uneasy after rich or heavy meals.
