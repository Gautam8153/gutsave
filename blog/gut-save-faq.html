What is Gut Save and what is it for?

Gut Save is a natural daily supplement formulated to address the root cause of bloating, constipation and everyday digestive discomfort, rather than only covering up symptoms after a heavy meal.

What is inside Gut Save?

The formula combines digestive enzymes, probiotic strains, prebiotic fiber and soothing botanicals such as ginger, peppermint, fennel, psyllium husk, aloe vera and licorice root. Check the label on the official order page for the exact amounts per capsule.

How do I take Gut Save?

The standard routine is 1 capsule per day, taken consistently. Because it is a daily formula rather than an occasional rescue product, the intended approach is to take it every day rather than only when bloating flares up.

How long before I notice a difference?

Some users report feeling lighter within the first week or two, while the deeper bacterial and regularity support is generally described as building over four to eight weeks of consistent daily use.

Is Gut Save safe, and are there side effects?

It is made from natural ingredients and is generally well tolerated, but fiber and probiotic formulas can cause mild gas or loose stools in the first few days as your gut adjusts. Talk to your doctor before starting if you are pregnant, nursing, on medication or managing a digestive condition.

Who should not take Gut Save?

Anyone under 18, women who are pregnant or breastfeeding, and people with a diagnosed digestive disorder or those taking prescription medication should consult a healthcare professional before use. If you are allergic to any botanical on the label, avoid it.
